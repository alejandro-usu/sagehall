        About Sage Hall

        Sage Hall opened in 2021, started by USU alum Josh Lyman and longtime dance instructor Manuel Carias-Pineda. The goal was simple: be the place in Logan where people meet, dance, and make some of their best memories.

        The name comes straight from the Aggie fight song, The Scotsman:

        “Show me a true blooded Aggie from Utah, who doesn't love the spot where the sagebrush grows!”
